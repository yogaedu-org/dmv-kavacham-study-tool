Sources

Every source behind the chapters, grouped by chapter. Dates are the publication or last-update date where the research reports gave one; otherwise the page was fetched on 2026-11-12 and no date was recorded.

Two kinds of claim appear in this book. A claim that carries a link was read or measured by the research reports. A claim marked hypothesis is the reports' own label for something not checked, and it is called that here too.

The research behind the whole book

  • Report 1: record and compare chanting in the browser, 2026-11-12. Record and compare, the two tiers, measured numbers and the calibration design.
  • Report 2: swappable per-verse audio, 2026-11-12. Cloud, offline and custom sources. Issue dmv-kavacham-study-tool #56.
  • Method of the chant accuracy analysis (14 verses over 6 days), 2026-11-11, with the confidence section added the same day.
  • dtw_probe.json and zipa_gop_probe.json: the measurements behind chapters 3 and 6, 2026-11-12. The scripts that produced them stay in the hub repository, which is not public from here.
  • The syllable boundaries and per-day confidences come from the analysis' results.json, summarised into chant.json by tools/build-chant-json.py. The recordings are not in the repository.
