How sure, at every level

Score says how alike your take is to the reference. Confidence says how much evidence the measurement had.

When confidence is under 30 the score is greyed and says "not enough to judge": it is not a mark. Try a long, clear recording of the verse before reading anything into it.

What this can and cannot tell you

It can tell you which syllables sounded unlike the reference, were held much longer or shorter than the rest of your take, or sat at a different pitch, and how much evidence each number rests on. It cannot tell you that you chanted wrongly: a difference may be your regular way of saying a syllable, a recording problem, or the tool's own habit. It has only been tested with one voice (chapter 8). The similarity and confidence numbers are estimates whose weights were not fitted to human judgements, and the thresholds behind the marks (1.6 times length, 150 cents) are hypotheses. Overall is the mean over the verses you have compared in this browser session.
