Lab 3: calibration

Plant a slip you know about in a reference clip, then see whether the tool flags it. Each try adds to a running tally.

The slip is made on a copy of the reference clip in your browser: either one syllable is silenced, or it is overwritten with a different syllable from the same clip. Then the damaged copy is compared with the untouched reference, just like a take in Lab 2. The planted-slip test on real takes is in chapter 7.

What this can and cannot tell you

It can tell you that the tool notices an obvious change in a clip it can compare with itself. It cannot tell you how often it will catch a real slip. The damaged copy has the same voice, microphone and room as the reference, so this is the easiest case there is; "N of M" here is not a recall rate on real mistakes. The measured test on real takes put the quick check's strongest mark on the slip in 26 of 26 cases, but only 19 of the 26 scored above the highest score a clean take got (chapter 7). A clean clip compared with itself should show no flags; if it does, that is a bug.
