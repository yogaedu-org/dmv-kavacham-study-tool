What we do not know yet

A method is only as good as the doubts written next to it. Here are the ones we know of, the biggest one, and the test that would settle it.

The biggest unknown: another voice

The reference and every test take are the same chanter. The quick check compares the colour of the sound, which carries the voice. The deeper check should cope better with a new voice, but on the one voice tested it already merges two sounds and drops a breath. Both statements are hypothesis until a live test.

Every other limit

  • The slips were cut in. A splice leaves sharp edges a real slip does not, which probably makes it easier to find.
  • The syllable moments are an automatic estimate, not a person's marking.
  • Some checks failed on some clips. One tool's catch-all sound won every moment of one clip, so it had to be ignored, and Whisper looped on two clips; a looped clip's result is void.
  • Edges. Ignore the first and last syllable unless the clip starts or ends mid-sound: one clean take spiked on its first.
  • No false-alarm rate. There are no human-labelled clean takes, so how often a clean take is wrongly marked has not been measured.
  • An earlier smoke test found four of five slips but placed a repeated stretch several syllables away. Five clips is not a recall rate.
  • Not built or not measured: the deeper check's browser audio features, and its speed in a browser.
  • iPhone storage. Safari deletes a site's stored data after 7 days without a visit, so recordings must be exported (source).

The test that is still missing

Three chanters, at least one who is not the author and one of the other sex, each chant five verses twice: once clean, and once from a card that names one slip in advance. The slips: an omission, tha for ta, ś for s, an insertion, a one-second pause, and a rushed half-length line. Count how many are found of how many, by type, and how many false marks a clean verse gets. Fix the thresholds on two chanters and test on the third. Lab 3 is a first, easy version of the same tally.

What this can and cannot tell you

It tells you what to doubt. It does not make any of it go away: until the live test is done, treat the tools as a second pair of ears, not a judge.
