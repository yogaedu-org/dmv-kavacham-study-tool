Lab 1: record and play back

Record yourself chanting a verse, or load a recording you already have, and hear it back. Nothing leaves this device.

Read from the ribbon, press the reference play button to hear the verse first if you like, then press Record. This lab only records and shows the sound. Lab 2 compares it with the reference. The background is in chapter 2.

What this can and cannot tell you

It can show that the recording worked, how long it is, whether the microphone settings the browser actually used are the ones we asked for, and where your pauses are. It cannot tell you anything about correctness: that is Lab 2. Your take is kept in this browser only; on an iPhone, Safari deletes stored data after 7 days without a visit, so keep a copy of anything you want to keep.
