Did I chant this verse the way the reference does, and where did I drift?

This book is about that one question and how far a computer can help answer it. Press play and watch the verse.

looking for audio

This is the ribbon, and it comes back in every chapter. Each cell is one syllable, placed where the reference chants it. The band under it is loudness and the line is pitch.

The answer in three sentences

A quick check lines your take up with a reference take using only the sound, so it needs no download, and shows which syllables you stretched, shortened or dropped and where your pitch moved. A deeper check downloads a sound model once (about 70 MB) that has learned the sounds of many languages and listens to each syllable on its own. On 26 slips planted in recordings of one chanter, the quick check put its top mark on the slip every time and the deeper check 25 times out of 26; but only one voice has been tested, and that is the biggest thing we do not know yet.

Source: research report 1, "Answer", measured 2026-11-12. Chapters 7 and 8 give the details and the limits.

    How this book works

    • Each chapter has one idea and ends with a "try it" that opens the matching tool in the lab.
    • Under every interactive element you can mark it Keep, Improve or Drop and leave a note. It is saved on this device, and the footer exports it as a file.
    • A dotted underline marks a specialist word: hover, tap or tab to it for a plain meaning.
    • If the ribbon says no audio was found, the message names where it looked and the one command that fixes it.
