      What this can and cannot tell you
      
    
        
  • Hypothesis: the timing ratio, spectral mismatch and pitch deviation below are computed from a speech-style alignment (MFCC and DTW) of your take against one reference clip. None of the thresholds was fitted to human judgements of right and wrong chanting.
    • 
        
  • Hypothesis: a flag means "this akshara differs from the Day 2 clip", not "you chanted it wrongly". A different but valid way of chanting, a different breath or a different mic can flag too.
    • 
        
  • The confidence beside each number is an estimate of how much evidence it rests on (how long the akshara is, how many voiced frames, how well it matched), not a probability.
    • 
        
  • Pitch is shown relative to your own key: the whole-take offset is removed so a different voice is not an error. A real octave change is not told apart from a key change.
    • 
        
  • Verse 14: its closing line repeats a variable number of times, so only line 1 is compared.
    • 
        
  • The calibration check below is a smoke test on synthetic edits of the reference itself, not a measure of how well it works on real mistakes.
